Bedrock server: operators, the allow list and bans are managed below; what Bedrock has no equivalent for is hidden.

  • Operators live in permissions.json, keyed by XUID. The panel records a player's XUID the first time they connect, so a player must have joined once before you can set their permission — or you can enter their XUID directly.
  • Bans are kept and enforced by the panel, because Bedrock has no ban list: a banned player is kicked as they connect. The ban therefore only holds while the server runs under the panel.
  • IP bans aren't possible — Bedrock never puts a client IP on the console.
  • Player data lives inside the world's LevelDB (worlds/<level>/db/), not per-player files.

🚫 Banned Players

Bedrock has no ban list of its own, so the panel keeps this one and enforces it by kicking a banned player as they connect. That means the ban only holds while the server runs under the panel, and the player is kicked just after joining rather than blocked at the door — for a hard block, use the allow list.
